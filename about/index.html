Family-owned craftsmanship

Furniture work built on practical judgment

Livingston Enterprises serves Central Pennsylvania from Newmanstown with decades of hands-on experience in wood finishing, repair and restoration.

Repair the right thing—not everything

Customers often arrive knowing what bothers them but not which process will solve it. A loose chair, cloudy tabletop and painted antique require different decisions. Livingston’s first responsibility is to identify the useful scope before beginning visible finish work.

That practical approach extends from everyday household furniture to family antiques and architectural doors. The business serves homeowners, collectors, historic properties and commercial clients across Lancaster, Lebanon, Berks, Dauphin, York and surrounding counties.

A direct conversation

You can call and describe the piece, or email photographs before arranging an assessment. We discuss condition, intended use, desired appearance and logistics. If a limited repair makes more sense than a complete refinish, that should be part of the recommendation.

What we value

  • Preserving sound material and construction
  • Explaining tradeoffs before work begins
  • Matching treatment to the piece rather than a package
  • Keeping heirlooms functional and recognizable
  • Using finishes appropriate for expected wear

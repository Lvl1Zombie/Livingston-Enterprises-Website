Architectural woodwork

Wood door refinishing in Central Pennsylvania

Older doors often contain better lumber and more character than replacements. When paint buildup, weather or a failing clear coat hides that value, careful refinishing can bring the wood forward again.

Interior and exterior door restoration

Doors are furniture under harder conditions. They are touched every day, exposed along edges, fitted with hardware and—outside—subjected to sunlight, moisture and temperature changes. Successful refinishing requires attention to all faces and to the environment the door will return to.

Removing accumulated paint and finish

Architectural doors may carry generations of paint in profiles, panel corners and around old hardware. Livingston has restored doors with as many as eight accumulated coats. Removal is planned to protect crisp edges and shaped details while revealing the condition of the wood below.

Not every painted door was originally intended for a clear natural finish. Species, repairs, patches and previous alterations can become visible only after stripping. The assessment should account for that possibility before the final appearance is promised.

Exterior exposure changes the finish plan

Sun and moisture attack exterior doors unevenly. Overhang, compass direction, storm-door heat and maintenance history all matter. A suitable exterior system can improve durability, but no clear exterior finish is maintenance-free. Periodic inspection and renewal are less costly than waiting for widespread failure.

Damage and hardware

Pet scratches, dents, open joints and damaged edges are considered before surface preparation. Hardware removal can expose protected color that helps guide restoration. If glass, specialty hardware or installation work requires another trade, that scope should be coordinated before the door leaves service.

What to send for an estimate

  • A photograph of the entire door from both sides
  • Close views of peeling, scratches and weathered areas
  • Door dimensions and number of doors
  • Whether the door is interior or exterior
  • Town and any scheduling constraints
